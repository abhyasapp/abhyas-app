The Level 7 Civil Engineering paper

Civil Engineering Loksewa preparation goes wrong when candidates study without checking the weight of each group. The written paper rewards balanced coverage: structures and geotechnical questions carry the largest block, but water resources, transportation, public health and professional practice together decide a large share of the marks.

Chapter-wise Civil Engineering question bank

Abhyas follows the Level 7 Civil Engineering syllabus and keeps General Knowledge alongside the technical chapters. The technical side is organised into ten chapters, each with its own sets of objective questions and a separate written-answer bank.

Structural Engineering

RCC, steel and timber members, seismic detailing, bridge design and the codes that govern safe structures.

Engineering Survey

Levelling, traverse, curves, modern instruments and the field calculations that appear in objective papers.

Construction Materials

Cement, aggregate, concrete, steel, timber and the tests that decide whether a material is fit for work.

Concrete Technology

Mix design, workability, curing, durability, admixtures and the reasons concrete fails in the field.

Geotechnical Engineering

Soil classification, compaction, consolidation, bearing capacity, foundations and slope stability.

Construction Management

Planning, scheduling, contracts, safety, quality control and the decisions that keep a site moving.

Estimating & Costing

Quantity take-off, rate analysis, specification and the arithmetic that turns drawings into a real cost.

Engineering Drawing

Projection, sections, scales and reading construction drawings accurately under exam time pressure.

Engineering Economics

Interest, present worth, depreciation, benefit-cost analysis and comparing competing project choices.

Professional Practices

Ethics, public procurement, contract administration, liability and the professional standards expected of an engineer.

General Knowledge covers Nepal's geography, history, constitution, public administration, current affairs, science and technology. It is practised in the same daily loop, so it does not get pushed to the last week.

How to practise, not just read

Most candidates lose marks to questions they have already read. The Abhyas Loksewa app is built around active recall instead of passive revision.

  1. Pick a chapter. Start with what you are covering today, or let the plan choose the next unfinished chapter.
  2. Answer a small set. Ten focused questions are enough to expose a weak concept without turning the day into a marathon.
  3. Review the explanation. Every question explains the correct option, so a wrong answer becomes a short lesson instead of a lost mark.
  4. Let missed questions return. The spaced-review queue brings them back days apart until the idea stays correct.
  5. Write under time. Use the daily written question and the full 100-mark paper to practise structure, handwriting and the upload workflow before exam day.

A 60-day Civil Loksewa study plan that adapts

A fixed timetable breaks the first time work, family or load-shedding interrupts a day. Abhyas recalculates instead of pretending the missed day did not happen.

  • Choose your topics. Mix Level 7, Level 5 and General Knowledge, or keep the plan to one level.
  • Set the exam date. The plan spreads every unseen question across the study days that remain.
  • Study weaker chapters earlier. Chapters with lower recorded accuracy move ahead of stronger ones.
  • Keep a revision buffer. The last two Learn days stay free of new questions for review, drills and weak spots.
  • See the truth early. Expected versus actual coverage shows whether you are ahead, on track or behind before the last week.

When a day is missed, the remaining questions are re-spread over the days that are left. If the required daily load becomes unrealistic, the app says so plainly rather than hiding the gap.

Civil Engineering Loksewa questions

What is the Civil Engineering Loksewa exam?

It is Nepal's Lok Sewa Aayog recruitment exam for Civil Engineering posts. At Level 7 it tests technical Civil Engineering knowledge, General Knowledge and written communication.

How many marks is the Level 7 written paper?

The written paper is 100 marks, organised into four groups: Structure and Geotechnical Engineering (30), Water Resource and Irrigation (25), Transportation Engineering (25), and Public Health, Project Management and Professional Practice (20). Always confirm the current marks in the official notice.

Is Abhyas the same as Abhya Loksewa?

Yes. Students type the name as Abhyas Loksewa, Abhya Loksewa or Abhyas Loksewa app. They all refer to the same Civil Engineering practice app described on this page.

Can I study offline?

Yes. Open a chapter once while online and its questions are saved on the device. After that the chapter opens with no connection, including during load-shedding or while travelling.

Does it include written-answer practice?

Yes. There is a timed written question every day and full 100-mark papers. You write on paper, upload one PDF, and a person marks it and sends feedback.

How should I start a 60-day plan?

Pick the chapters you want to cover, set your exam date, and let Abhyas spread the unseen questions across the study days. The plan keeps the last two Learn days for revision, schedules weaker chapters earlier and re-plans automatically when you miss a day.
